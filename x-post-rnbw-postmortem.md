RNBW postmortem — the CCA that nobody's talking about.

Rainbow ran a Continuous Clearing Auction on Base in February. Here's how it went:

Floor: $0.10 (FDV $100M)
Clearing: $0.11 (FDV $111M, 11% above floor)
Raised: $330K USDC
Bidders: 1,526 unique wallets
Duration: 72 hours
Result: Graduated

Current price: ~$0.013. That's 86% below auction clearing.

CCA gives you fair entry — everyone pays the same clearing price. But fair entry doesn't mean fair exit. The mechanism works exactly as designed. What happens after graduation is market.

52 wallets that bid in AZTEC also bid in RNBW. That's the biggest overlap of any auction pair — even across chains (mainnet to Base). CCA has a repeat audience.
